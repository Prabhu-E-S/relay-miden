## Prompt / Objective

The user enabled local-development camera access on their phone, but the in-app scanner did not recognize Relay Miden QR codes that the native camera application could read as JSON text. Improve the live scanner's recognition reliability.

Improved PaperBridge live camera scanning for dense signed JSON QR envelopes. The scanner no longer limits decoding to a 240×240 crop. It uses a preferred high-resolution rear-camera stream, scans the full frame, and limits decoding to QR symbols. QR cards are larger on laptop and desktop displays to keep modules sufficiently large for phone cameras.
